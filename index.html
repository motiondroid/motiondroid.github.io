How I work

A simple four-step flow — you always know what’s happening and when.

01 DAY 0

Brief & footage

Share the raw footage (a Drive link works), a reference video you like and your deadline. I confirm scope and price before starting.

02 2–3 DAYS

First cut

I cut for story and pacing first — hook, structure, B-roll, captions, music and sound cleanup.

03 2 ROUNDS

Revisions

You leave timestamped notes, I refine the cut. Two revision rounds are included.

04 FINAL FILE

Delivery

Final export in the right format for YouTube, Shorts or Reels — ready to upload.
