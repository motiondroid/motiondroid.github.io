How I work

Four clear steps — you always know what’s happening and when.

i.Day 0

Brief & footage

Share the raw footage (a Drive link works), a reference you like and your deadline. Scope and price are confirmed before I start.

ii.2–3 days

First cut

Story and pacing first — hook, structure, B-roll, captions, music and sound cleanup.

iii.2 rounds

Revisions

You leave timestamped notes, I refine the cut. Two revision rounds are included.

iv.Final

Delivery

Final export in the right format for YouTube, Shorts or Reels — ready to upload.
